Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a weekly pool service visit?

Every visit includes skimming, brushing walls and tile, vacuuming, emptying skimmer and pump baskets, and on-site water testing with adjustments to pH, chlorine, and alkalinity. You get a report after each visit covering chemistry, equipment, and any recommendations.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners across the Las Vegas Valley, including North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, and Boulder City. Contact us to confirm coverage for your neighborhood.

Can you clean a green pool?

Yes. Our green-to-clean recovery service uses shock treatment, filtration, and algae removal to bring a neglected or green pool back to swim-ready.

Do I need to be home for my pool service?

No. Your technician shows up on schedule, and you do not need to be home. The same tech visits on the same day each week, so nothing falls through the cracks.

How do I get a free quote?

Send us a few details about your pool through the contact form or call (725) 710-5199. We schedule a free on-site assessment, then recommend a weekly plan or one-time service based on your pool's condition.

Do you offer one-time services as well as weekly plans?

Yes. Besides weekly service, we offer green-to-clean recovery, filter cleaning and replacement, pool draining, acid and chlorine wash, and seasonal openings and closings.

Do you clean spas and hot tubs?

Yes. We clean attached spas and hot tubs and balance their chemistry as part of your regular pool visit.

Will you try to sell me extra repairs?

No. If a fix isn't worth it, we'll tell you. No upselling, ever.

Still have a question?

Call or send us a message. We're happy to look at your pool and give you straight answers.
