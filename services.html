Services & Pricing

One team for cleaning, service, and everything in between

Pick a weekly plan or book a one-time service — every job includes water testing and a full report.

Weekly Pool Cleaning

Skimming, brushing walls and tile, vacuuming, and emptying skimmer & pump baskets on a set day every week.

Chemical Balancing

On-site water testing with precise adjustments to pH, chlorine, and alkalinity so water stays safe and clear.

Green-to-Clean Recovery

Shock treatment, filtration, and algae removal that brings a neglected or green pool back to swim-ready.

Filter Cleaning & Replacement

Cartridge, DE, and sand filter cleaning or replacement so your system filters at full capacity.

Seasonal Openings & Closings

Start-of-season startups and end-of-season closures to protect your pool and equipment year-round.

Spa & Hot Tub Care

Attached spa cleaning and chemical balancing bundled right into your regular pool visit.

Acid & Chlorine Wash

A drained-pool deep clean using diluted acid and chlorine to strip algae stains, calcium buildup, and discoloration from plaster or pebble surfaces, leaving a bright, even finish.

Pool Draining

Full or partial draining to prep your pool for an acid wash, resurfacing, or other major work — handled carefully to protect your equipment, decking, and surrounding landscaping.
